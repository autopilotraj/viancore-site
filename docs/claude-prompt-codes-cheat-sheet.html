Claude Prompt Codes Cheat Sheet

Short codes you type at the start of a prompt to change how Claude responds, grouped by purpose. Example: BLUF NOFLUFF What is the difference between a Roth and a traditional IRA?

Top 10 Codes to Learn First
CodeWhat it doesUse it for
TLDRPuts a one or two line summary at the topLong answers you want to skim
ELI5Explains simply, with an analogy and no jargonUnfamiliar topics
L99Expert-level depth that commits to one recommendationDecisions where you want a clear call
OODAStructures the answer as Observe, Orient, Decide, ActMessy or fast-moving situations
BLUFBottom line up front, then the supporting detailStatus updates and executive answers
STEPBYSTEPReasons through the problem step by step before answeringLogic, math and troubleshooting
REDTEAMAttacks your idea or plan to expose weak pointsPlans before you commit
PERSONAAnswers as a named expert (PERSONA: tax attorney)Specialist viewpoints
BLINDSPOTPoints out what you haven't considered or askedBig decisions
GHOSTRewrites in natural, human-sounding proseDrafts that sound machine-written
Thinking & Reasoning
CodeWhat it doesUse it for
DEEPTHINKSlows down and reasons thoroughly before answeringHard or high-stakes questions
FIRSTPRINCIPLESBreaks the problem down to fundamentals and rebuildsChallenging conventional wisdom
SECONDORDERMaps the knock-on effects of a decisionStrategy, policy, career moves
INVERTAsks how this would fail and works backwardsProblem-solving when stuck
PARETOFinds the 20% of actions driving 80% of resultsPrioritizing
UNPACKBreaks a complex idea into its partsDense topics
LAYEREDGives a basic, intermediate and expert answerMixed audiences
MECESplits a topic into non-overlapping, complete bucketsStructuring problems and decks
ASSUMELists the assumptions behind the answer firstEstimates and business cases
STEELMANGives the strongest form of the opposing viewBefore arguing a position
Learning & Explaining
CodeWhat it doesUse it for
ELI10Plain language with a bit more detail than ELI5Non-technical audiences
SCAFFOLDTeaches in layers (basics, concepts, advanced) and checks understanding at each stepLearning a subject properly
TEACHMETurns the answer into a structured lessonSelf-study
SOCRATICTeaches by asking you questions instead of tellingReally owning an idea
ANALOGYExplains through a comparison to something familiarBridging to a new field
EXAMPLEAdds a concrete, realistic worked exampleAbstract explanations
CRASHCOURSEThe essentials in about five minutes of readingGetting up to speed fast
GAPFINDERProbes what you know and finds the gapsExam or interview prep
DRILLGenerates practice exercisesRetention
QUIZEnds with 3 to 5 questions to test youChecking understanding
Output & Format
CodeWhat it doesUse it for
BRIEFUnder 100 words, no preambleQuick answers, mobile
NOFLUFFNo intro, recap, disclaimers or fillerAnswers that feel padded
TABLEPuts the answer in a comparison tableOptions, products, vendors
CHECKLISTOrdered [ ] action itemsProjects, audits, launches
STEPSNumbered how-to with exact actionsSetup and configuration
COMPARESide-by-side comparison that names a winnerBuying and tool decisions
DIGESTSummarizes the essentials of long contentReports, articles, transcripts
EXTRACTPulls only specific facts from a documentContracts, data, research
SOURCESCites a link for every factual claimAnything you'll repeat to others
RAWPlain text, no markdown formattingPasting into other apps
Writing & Tone
CodeWhat it doesUse it for
POLISHLifts a rough draft to professional qualityEmails, reports, posts
TRIMCuts length while keeping the meaningWordy drafts
PUNCHMakes sentences more direct and forcefulWeak or hedging copy
MIRRORMatches your writing style from a sample you pasteWriting in your own voice
DRAFTReturns only ready-to-send textEmails and messages
HOOKWrites a strong opening linePosts, articles, talks
REPHRASESays the same thing in completely new wordsRepetitive text
FORMAL / CASUALShifts the register up or downMatching the audience
Critique & Decisions
CodeWhat it doesUse it for
PREMORTEMAssumes the plan failed in a year and explains whyProjects and launches
DEVILArgues against your current leaningChecking confirmation bias
SWOTStrengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threatsBusiness and career options
DECIDEPicks one option and defends it, with a runner-upBreaking a deadlock
SCORERates your work 1 to 10 against clear criteria, with fixesResumes, proposals, pitches
AUDITReviews your work and flags issuesDocuments, code, processes
FACTCHECKChecks claims and flags anything doubtfulBefore publishing
BOTTLENECKFinds the one constraint holding things backStalled projects
WARGAMESimulates how a competitor or counterpart would respondNegotiations and strategy
CONFIDENCEEnds with a confidence level and what would change itUncertain topics
Power Combos
ComboWhat you get
OODA L99A committed, expert-level decision on a messy situation
ELI5 SCAFFOLD QUIZLearn a new topic in layers, then test yourself
BLUF NOFLUFFThe shortest useful answer
REDTEAM PREMORTEMA full stress test before you commit
PERSONA TABLEAn expert's side-by-side comparison
POLISH TRIMA tighter, cleaner version of your draft
DEEPTHINK SOURCESA careful answer you can verify

Stick to two or three codes per prompt. Avoid pairs that pull in opposite directions, such as BRIEF with DEEPTHINK or ELI5 with L99.
